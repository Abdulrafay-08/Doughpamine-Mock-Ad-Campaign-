Infographic 2: Step-by-Step Keyword Research Process for Google Ads / PPC Campaigns

Ad groups segment keywords for relevance, with 5–10 keywords per group.

  1. Cookies Group: Focus on cookie dough and gourmet cookies. Keywords: gourmet cookies Karachi, frozen cookie dough, buy cookie dough online.
  2. Cakes Group: Custom and birthday cakes. Keywords: custom cakes near me, birthday cakes Karachi, gourmet cakes delivery.
  3. Bread & Pastries Group: Fresh bread and pastries. Keywords: fresh bread bakery, artisanal pastries Karachi, buy bread online.
  4. General Bakery Group: Broad searches. Keywords: best bakery Karachi, bakery near me, doughpamine bakers.

This structure allows tailored bidding and ads, improving Quality Score.
